# Privacy Policy – StickMindR8 React

Last updated: 2026-11-13

StickMindR8 React is a personal tool used by the owner of the StickMindR8 channel to publish his own short animated videos to his own TikTok account.

**What we access.** When the owner connects his TikTok account, the app receives his basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) and permission to upload and publish videos to that same account.

**What we store.** The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. Access tokens are stored only by the owner's own automation tools and are used solely to publish his own videos.

**Third parties.** Data is not shared with third parties, except the services needed to publish the videos (TikTok).

**Deleting access.** The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services.

**Contact.** StickMindR8 – via the channel on YouTube (@r8stickmind).
