# What If - idea backlog (not yet produced)

Proposed 2026-10-30 (shorts <= 45 s, twist every ~8-10 s, ending tags a specific friend). Idea 7 was chosen and built as 09_billionaire_one_day.

1. What If Gravity Turned Off for 10 Seconds? - hang hook: Stick floats over the city; point: gravity returns over an empty pool. Ending: send to the friend who'd float away first.
2. What If You Could Clone Yourself 3 Times? - clones work, sleep, complain; punch: they form a union and ask for a salary. Ending: send to your laziest clone.
3. What If Your Shadow Had a Mind of Its Own? - shadow stops mimicking, leaves, saves Stick from a car; punch: note "you're welcome". Ending: tag the friend whose shadow is smarter.
4. What If Your Phone Battery Was Your Life? - 1% = 1 minute; punch: phone on silent, lives another day. Ending: send to whoever scrolls at 3 AM.
5. What If You Had Three Wishes, But the Genie Was a Lawyer? - clauses on every wish; punch: wish 3 cancels wish 1. Ending: send to the friend who reads every terms and conditions.
6. What If You Woke Up as the Last Person on Earth, and the Phone Rang? - empty city, unknown number; punch: it's mom asking why he isn't at work. Ending: send to your mom.
8. What If You Could Teleport, But Only to Your Most Embarrassing Moments? - each jump worse; punch: the last jump is a new embarrassment from today. Ending: send to the friend who still remembers yours.
9. What If Your Superpower Was the Weakest One Ever? - talks to pigeons; punch: the pigeon saves him and wants bread. Ending: send to your hero friend.
10. What If You Found a Door to Yesterday and Met Yourself? - he gives himself bad advice; punch: he is the one who ruined yesterday. Ending: send to the friend who gives bad advice.
